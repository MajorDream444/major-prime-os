# BWYH — LEAD FLOW (Build While You Heal)

Stage 1 — Discovery:
- Scout identifies medspa owners or high-ticket buyers
- Firecrawl pulls contact + business data

Stage 2 — Audit:
- Pull website, socials, reviews
- Score opportunity (high / medium / low)

Stage 3 — Outreach:
- Draft personalized message
- Send via Telegram or WhatsApp
- Log touchpoint in Airtable

Stage 4 — Close:
- Follow up sequence (3-touch)
- Move to Notion deal board
- Track revenue outcome
